**Demoted-from:** BLOCKING
**Location:** batch 4 / card 13
**Issue:** The opening sentence says "the reap fires on that test's second `lyx reed up` and kills both the unadopted initial pane and the foreign pane", while the later pane-count paragraph correctly says "the reap fires on the *first* `up`, so the initial pane is already gone and those counts become 1 and 2".
