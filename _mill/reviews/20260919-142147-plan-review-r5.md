## Findings

### [BLOCKING:scope] shedcli `entry` has no `Verbs` field
**Location:** batch 5, card 29 (against cards 30 and 31)
**Issue:** Card 29 declares `type entry struct` with exactly two fields — `Arm` and `Args` — and a two-key `recipes` literal filling only those, but card 30 requires the pre-run to "refuse on the envelope when `cmd.Name()` is not in that entry's `Verbs` set" and card 31 asserts "every entry's `Verbs` set is a subset of the four verbs ... `loom` carries all four while `lifecycle` carries three"; the field the other two cards read is never declared or filled.
**Fix:** Card 29 must declare the `Verbs` field on `entry` and fill it per recipe (loom: run/step/status/pause; lifecycle: run/status/pause), since it is the sole authority the verb-set refusal and the table test both read.

### [NIT:consistency] Two shedverbs helpers are named twice, with different casing
**Demoted-from:** BLOCKING
**Location:** batch 3, cards 12 and 14 (against batch 3's scope decision and batch 4, card 23)
**Issue:** Batch 3's scope decision and card 23 name `shedverbs.RenderStatusLine` as one of the four **exported** helpers `internal/loomcli/status_test.go` retargets onto, but card 14 declares it as `renderStatusLine(label, st)` — unexported and unreachable from `loomcli` (verified: `internal/loomcli/status_test.go:58` calls `renderStatusLine(tt.st)` in-package today). Card 12 has the same defect internally: it declares "**exported** `StepEnvelope(res, nextPolicy, statusFile)`" and then has the success path call `stepEnvelope(res, nextPolicy, spec.StatusPath)`.
**Fix:** Pin one casing per helper across every card — `RenderStatusLine` and `StepEnvelope` exported, matching the batch's own exported-four decision and card 23's retargeting instruction.

### [BLOCKING:design] Batch 6's verify rationale rests on a false claim about `constraintchokepoint_test.go`
**Location:** batch 6, card 39 and its `## Batch Tests`
**Issue:** Both state that `cmd/lyx/constraintchokepoint_test.go` "reads `CONSTRAINTS.md`'s structure" and that it is why `./cmd/lyx/...` is in `verify:`. Verified against that file's own doc comment and its `glyphChokepointViolation`/`isGlyphUnitAsPathLine`/`glyphUnitPathContextTokens` constructs: it enforces the **Glyph Conversion Chokepoint Invariant** by scanning production `.go` source for glyph↔path conversion and never opens `CONSTRAINTS.md`. A repo-wide grep finds no `_test.go` that parses `CONSTRAINTS.md` at all.
**Fix:** Drop the claim and state plainly that card 35's new section carries no mechanical structure guard — its enforcement is the three tests card 35 already names plus review discipline.

### [BLOCKING:scope] Batch 4 rebuilds both live subtrees but its verify never runs `cmd/lyx`
**Location:** batch 4 `verify:` (against cards 24, 26 and 28)
**Issue:** Batch 4's `verify:` covers `internal/loomcli`, `internal/lifecyclecli` and `internal/shedverbs` only, yet it is the batch that replaces `loomcli.Command()`'s four `AddCommand` arguments and `lifecyclecli.Command()`'s with `shedverbs.Verbs(...)` output. Card 24 names `cmd/lyx/drift_test.go`'s blank-`Short` check as "the one part that is machine-checked", and card 26 reasons explicitly about lifecycle's zero-valued `step` texts never reaching the tree that guard walks — but nothing in this batch runs it, so a lost `Short` or a mis-selected returned command surfaces only in batch 5.
**Fix:** Add `./cmd/lyx/...` to batch 4's `verify:` (batch 5 and 6 already run it, so this is no scope escalation).

### [NIT:consistency] Card 10 says "five" hooks and then lists six
**Location:** batch 3, card 10
**Issue:** "Declare `type Hooks struct` with five nil-by-default function fields" is immediately followed by a six-item list (`PreRun`, `PostRun`, `PreStep`, `PostStep`, `InterruptPolicyFor`, `StatusExtras`), which is what the overview's `poststep-is-a-sixth-hook` Shared Decision fixes.

### [NIT:consistency] Cards 22/23 leave loomcli non-compiling until card 24

Each card carries its own `Commit:`.
**Fix:** State which card owns the `Command()` rewiring, or fold the three into one commit boundary so no card's commit leaves the package uncompilable.
