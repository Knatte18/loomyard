Added `internal/gitrepo/gogit_test.go` to card 21's `Edits:`, named the three specific case groups that move (card 8's five states, card 9's linked-worktree run, card 19's `PushCoalesced` gating case), and stated explicitly that the file is edited **only if the reversal branch fires** — otherwise the card touches `push.go` alone.
