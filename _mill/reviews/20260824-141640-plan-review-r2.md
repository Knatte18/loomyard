### [NIT:design] Card 13's "typed-nil vs untyped-nil" test split is not a real distinction for these two fields
**Location:** batch 3, card 13 (`internal/shedrecipe/entries_discussionwrite_test.go`)
**Issue:** Card 13 asks for a separate "typed-nil" subtest per func field (`DiscussionSpec`, `CommitDiscussion`), "assigning a nil-valued variable of the field's own func type, not the untyped nil literal," so `requireSeam`'s reflect branch is "genuinely exercised rather than only its plain-nil branch." **Fix:** Drop the "typed-nil vs untyped-nil" distinction for `DiscussionSpec`/`CommitDiscussion` — one nil-value subtest per field suffices; if a reflect-branch-vs-plain-branch distinction is wanted anywhere, it belongs on the interface-typed `Shuttle` field instead.
