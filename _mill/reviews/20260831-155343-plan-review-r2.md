## Findings

### [BLOCKING:design] Card 8's "capture, never invent" premise is infeasible for 401/403 and unhandled for symlink/submodule
**Location:** batch 2, card 8 **Issue:** The card's stated method ("build every failure fixture from what actually came back") never describes how `error-401.json`/`error-403.json` are actually captured — you cannot produce a live 401 without revoking the very `gh auth` the rest of the card's captures depend on, and 403 (rate-limit/access-denied) isn't something to deliberately trigger. The card also treats `probe-symlink.json`/`probe-submodule.json`/the curl-symlink capture as unconditional `Creates:` and mandatory card-14 scenarios, while the capture list only says "if one exists in any reachable repository" for them and gives no fallback disposition (contrast the >1MB file, which explicitly gets a "record that it was not observed" fallback). **Fix:** State explicitly that 401/403/no-status/multiline bodies are derived from the pinned 404 shape (GitHub's documented error envelope) rather than live-captured, and give symlink/submodule/curl-symlink the same "if not found, record and carry as described" disposition already given to the >1MB case.

### [BLOCKING:scope] Batch 1 never assigns the two new manual-check notes to a card
**Location:** batch 1, Batch Tests section vs. cards 1–7 **Issue:** The batch narrative asserts "one live `--children` run... and one live guard trip... documented as manual checks in the same style as the harness's existing header note," but no card's Requirements says to add these to `github-tree-selftest.sh`'s header "NOT covered here" list — only card 4 extends that header, and only for the absolute-fixture mechanism/generator. Batch 2's card 13 does this correctly for its own harness (explicit "not covered here" note listing the live checks), so the omission in batch 1 is a real gap, not a stylistic choice. **Fix:** Add a sentence to card 4 (or a new bullet in card 5/6) requiring the two new manual-check lines be appended to the harness header's existing note.

### [NIT:consistency] Card 6's boundary scenario doesn't name a fixture
**Location:** batch 1, card 6, fourth scenario **Issue:** "assert that a ceiling exactly equal to the listing's entry count succeeds... and that a ceiling one lower aborts" never names which fixture/count establishes "the listing," unlike every other scenario in this card which is precisely pinned. **Fix:** Name the fixture (e.g. reuse `small-root-rec.json`'s three blobs, or a `gen_tree_body` count) explicitly.

### [NIT:consistency] Card 11's fallback-cost sentence is ambiguous
**Location:** batch 2, card 11 **Issue:** "Record in a comment that the probe's second call is paid only on the fallback path... and that its base64 content is downloaded and discarded" conflates "the probe" (first call) and "the second call" (raw-media fetch) in one clause, though the paragraph elsewhere clearly separates them. **Fix:** Reword to state plainly that both fallback calls, and the probe's wasted base64 payload, are paid only when raw already failed.

## Verdict

REQUEST_CHANGES
Card 8's live-capture premise is unworkable for two mandatory fixtures and batch 1 drops a promised doc note.
