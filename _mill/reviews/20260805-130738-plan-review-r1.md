## Findings

### [BLOCKING] Card 3 deletes `Layout.Repo` but tells tokenvocab to keep reading it
**Location:** 01-pre-moves.md, Card 3
**Issue:** Requirements delete the `Repo` field (grouped with `Prime`) from `hubgeometry.Layout`, yet the same paragraph says "Leave `tokenvocab`'s `c.Layout.Repo` expression alone — card 5 renames the field." `tokenvocab/tokenvocab.go:25` and six struct literals across `tokenvocab_test.go` read `.Repo` directly, and both files are already in this card's own `Edits:` list. Deleting the field without updating those reads breaks compilation, contradicting the batch's explicit claim that each of its four cards "leaves `go test ./...` green on its own."
**Fix:** Keep `Repo`/`deriveRepo` alive through card 3 (defer their removal to card 5, which already reshapes the type), or give tokenvocab's two files a stopgap read in card 3.

### [BLOCKING] Card 5's Moves list has a self-referential pair for anchor.go
**Location:** 02-rename-and-reshape.md, Card 5
**Issue:** The first `Moves:` entry reads `` `internal/lyxcwd/anchor.go` -> `internal/lyxcwd/anchor.go` `` — identical source and destination. Verified on disk: `internal/hubgeometry/anchor.go` exists today and `internal/lyxcwd` does not exist yet, so this pair is not merely mislabeled, it is a `git mv` of a nonexistent source, and no other Moves entry anywhere in the plan captures the real `hubgeometry/anchor.go → lyxcwd/anchor.go` rename (Cards 6/7/8 and later cards all assume `internal/lyxcwd/anchor.go` already exists).
**Fix:** Correct the pair to `` `internal/hubgeometry/anchor.go` -> `internal/lyxcwd/anchor.go` ``.

### [BLOCKING] Cards 6-7 reference pre-rename paths and the old type name after Card 5 already ran
**Location:** 02-rename-and-reshape.md, Cards 6 and 7
**Issue:** Card 5 (same batch, immediately prior) does `git mv internal/hubgeometry internal/lyxcwd`, renames `hubgeometry.go`→`lyxcwd.go`, and renames `Layout`→`Location`. Card 6's `Edits:` still names `internal/hubgeometry/hubgeometry.go` (should be `internal/lyxcwd/lyxcwd.go`), and its Requirements spec `ResolveWithAnchor(cwd, anchor string) (*Layout, error)` — should return `*Location`. Card 7's `Edits:` still names `internal/hubgeometry/anchor_test.go` (should be `internal/lyxcwd/anchor_test.go`).
**Fix:** Update both cards' `Edits:` lists to the post-rename paths and Card 6's signature to `*Location`.

### [NIT] Batch 2 mixes a single directory-level move with 17 per-file Move pairs
**Location:** 02-rename-and-reshape.md, Batch Scope + Card 5
**Issue:** Batch Scope frames the rename as one atomic `git mv internal/hubgeometry internal/lyxcwd`, but Card 5 also lists 17 individual per-file `Moves:` pairs and the boilerplate "Rename mechanic" section instructs running `git mv <old> <new>` per pair — two framings for the same operation that, combined with the malformed anchor.go pair above, could lead an implementer to attempt 17 separate commands instead of the one directory move.
**Fix:** State explicitly in Card 5 that the directory-level `git mv` is the single required operation and the per-file list documents its effect rather than being 17 separate commands.
