## Findings

### [BLOCKING:design] Batch 4 card 27 gives no per-site resolution for perchcli's three ad-hoc SeedConfig sites
**Location:** batch 4 (`04-small-consumers.md`), card 27, `internal/perchcli`
**Issue:** The overview's "three-way SeedConfig triage" decision names exactly three ad-hoc sites as the reason `hubforge.SeedConfig(tb, h, map)` takes only a `*Hub` and refuses a caller-supplied directory. Two of the three live in this card's own files — `cli_integration_test.go:108` (`lyxtest.SeedConfig(t, nested, …)`, `TestRunCLI_Pause_NestedInitAnchorsRunDirsAtCwd`) and `run_integration_test.go:207` (`lyxtest.SeedConfig(t, warpSubdir, …)`, `TestRunCLI_Run_FabricCommitExcludesLockFiles_NestedRelPath`) — yet card 27's Requirements only say "apply the SeedConfig triage to all six calls," which doesn't fit either site: neither seeds `h.WeftBase` or `h.BoardDir()`, both seed a hand-built nested/`relPath` subdirectory the new `hubforge.SeedConfig` signature cannot address. Both tests also hand-roll `seedFabricAnchor`/`seedRepoWideFabricConfig` helpers that write `.lyx-anchor` and repo-wide `fabric.yaml` by hand — the exact stand-in-hub hack batch 6 card 36 explicitly deletes for the analogous `loomengine` helper — but no equivalent instruction exists here, and no card tells the implementer whether the intended fix is `hubforge.NewHub(t, "nested")`/`hubforge.NewHub(t, "wts/some-task")` (letting the real hub produce the anchor for real) or something else.
**Fix:** Add explicit per-site Requirements to card 27 (or a new card) resolving both sites: state whether `hubforge.NewHub` should be called with the real nested/relPath anchor to replace the hand-rolled `.lyx-anchor`/`fabric.yaml` writing, or whether these two calls stay on a non-`hubforge` seeding path and why — matching the specificity batch 5 card 33 already gives for reedcli's own ad-hoc `sibling` site.
