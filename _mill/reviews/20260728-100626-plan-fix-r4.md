The discipline lived only in the overview's Shared Decisions and appeared in no card's requirements, while `goGit()` takes its write `Lock` around the cache-check/open step alone and returns an **unprotected** handle — so a caller reading outside the read lock races a concurrent open or reindex, and the two methods reading an unresolved reference directly (`CurrentBranch`, `remoteName`) are not covered by the lookup helper either. Took the reviewer's own single-point-of-truth option, since every migrating card already carries `gogit.go` in Context: card 1 now requires `goGit`'s godoc to state that callers must hold the read lock for the **whole duration of their use of the handle**, not merely across the `goGit()` call, and to say why the method cannot enforce it itself. Reinforced with a batch-local decision at the top of batches 3 and 4 marking it as applying to every card without exception, and named it explicitly at the two direct-reference reads where the helper gives no cover.
- `20260728-100517-plan-review-r4.md` — "[BLOCKING] hasUnpushed and SetSnapshotSHA's canonicalization skip the fingerprint helper": verified and accurate. Cards 11 and 16 route `SHAExists` and `isStrictDescendant` through the helper explicitly, but card 18 never said so for `hasUnpushed` — despite `hasUnpushed` being named in the Shared Decision as one of the three swallow-to-default methods the helper exists for — and card 17 tied only its adopted-ref read to the helper, leaving the `^{commit}` canonicalization unrouted. Card 18 now requires routing the `CommitObject` reads behind both ancestor walks, with the reason stated (it swallows into `true`, so it can never surface an `object not found` itself, and `PushCoalesced` calls it right after pack-writing CLI pushes). Card 17 now routes both of its reads, noting the canonicalization resolves a caller-supplied SHA to a commit immediately after the method's own CLI fetch — exactly the shape the gate exists for.
