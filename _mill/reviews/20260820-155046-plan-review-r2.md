## Findings

### [BLOCKING:design] Card 6's ShedProducer assertion precedes Card 8's Call, breaking intermediate builds
**Location:** Batch 3 / Card 6 (and Card 7)
**Issue:** Card 6 requires `var _ shedengine.ShedProducer = (*BurlerProducer)(nil)` right after the struct/constructor, but `shedengine.ShedProducer` requires a `Call(ctx context.Context) (Outcome, OutputPointer, error)` method, and `Call` is not implemented until Card 8. `go build`/`go vet` fails on the state left by Card 6's own commit and by Card 7's own commit (Card 7 adds only unexported helpers, no `Call`), violating each card's self-containment.
**Fix:** Move the `var _ shedengine.ShedProducer = (*BurlerProducer)(nil)` line from Card 6 into Card 8, placed with (or immediately after) the `Call` implementation, so every card's own commit leaves the package compiling.

### [NIT:consistency] Card 8's "fresh profile copy" rationale misattributes the reason
**Location:** Batch 3 / Card 8
**Issue:** The stated reason to build a fresh template-profile copy per round — "because burlerengine.Profile.validate mutates the profile in place and Engine.Run takes it by value" — is imprecise: since `Run(p Profile, ...)` takes `p` by value, `validate`'s in-place mutation of its own local copy can never propagate back to the caller's struct regardless of whether a fresh copy is used. The real reason is that each round must set its own `ReviewPath`/`FixerReportPath`/`PriorReviews`/`PriorFixerReports`/`ClusterExclude`, which would otherwise leak stale values across rounds.
**Fix:** Restate the rationale as needing fresh per-round field values, not as a workaround for `validate`'s mutation semantics.

## Verdict

Card 6's premature ShedProducer assertion breaks intermediate builds before Card 8 adds Call.
