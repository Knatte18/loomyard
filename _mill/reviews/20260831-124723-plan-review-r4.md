### [BLOCKING:scope] Cards 12/13 call `e.TmuxPath()` but `lock.go` is absent from Context

### [NIT:consistency] Card 2's "two strands" rationale doesn't match `FixedHeightPins`
**Location:** 01-dotfill-repro-harness.md, Card 2, step 5
**Issue:** The card justifies requiring two `addStrand` calls with "`render.FixedHeightPins` only produces pins for a multi-cell layout." Per `render/rules.go`, `FixedHeightPins` emits the header pin whenever `hasHeader` is true (i.e., whenever the always-present header pane exists) regardless of strand count, and two independent (non-parent/child) strands with no shrink relationship produce no additional strip pin either — so the header alone already yields a non-empty ("real") pin set with just one strand under this reasoning.
**Fix:** Either restate the rationale in terms of pane-count/round-robin fidelity (why 3 total panes reproduce the artifact better than 2) rather than pin-count, or confirm empirically and adjust the comment card 2 asks the implementer to write.
